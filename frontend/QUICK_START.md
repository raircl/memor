# Quick Start Guide - Receipt Photobooth

## For Samsung Galaxy Tab A11 Users

### Step 1: Hardware Setup (2 minutes)
1. **Connect Printer**
   - Plug the USB OTG cable into your Samsung Galaxy Tab A11
   - Connect the other end to your XP T80A printer
   - Turn on the printer
   - Load 80mm thermal paper into the printer

2. **Verify Connection**
   - Your tablet should recognize the USB device
   - You may see a notification about USB connection

### Step 2: App Installation (5 minutes)
1. **Download Expo Go**
   - Open Google Play Store on your tablet
   - Search for "Expo Go"
   - Install the Expo Go app
   - Open Expo Go

2. **Load the Photobooth App**
   - Scan the QR code provided
   - Or enter the URL manually in Expo Go
   - The app will load (may take 30-60 seconds first time)

### Step 3: First Time Setup (1 minute)
1. **Grant Camera Permission**
   - When prompted, tap "Grant Permission"
   - This allows the app to use your tablet's camera

2. **Confirm Printer Connection**
   - Look for "● Printer Ready" in green at the top
   - If it shows "● No Printer" in red, tap the 🔌 button

### Step 4: Start Using! (Immediate)
You're ready to go! Just:
1. Position yourself in the camera frame
2. Tap the big white button
3. Your photo prints automatically!

## Quick Reference

### Button Guide
```
🔄 (Left)   - Switch camera (front/back)
⚪ (Center) - Take photo & print
🔌 (Right)  - Reconnect printer
```

### Status Indicators
- **● Printer Ready** (Green) = Ready to print
- **● No Printer** (Red) = Printer not detected

### Each Print Shows:
- "PHOTO RECEIPT" header
- Current date and time
- Your photo (auto-sized)
- Auto-cuts when done

## Common Questions

**Q: How do I switch between front and back camera?**
A: Tap the 🔄 button on the left

**Q: What if the printer isn't detected?**
A: Tap the 🔌 button to reconnect, or unplug and replug the USB cable

**Q: Are photos saved?**
A: No, photos print immediately and are not saved anywhere

**Q: Can I preview before printing?**
A: No, it prints immediately (by design for speed)

**Q: How many photos can I print?**
A: As many as you want! Just keep feeding paper into the printer
